Questions buyers actually ask

Why not just buy a salary survey subscription?

Surveys give you data. They do not tell you what to pay your Operations Director in your sector at your size, and they cost more than a Benchmark before you have interpreted a single number. You are buying the judgement, footnoted, not another spreadsheet.

We are under 100 employees. Does the transparency agenda even apply to us?

The EU reporting thresholds start at 100 employees, but the right to pay information and the ban on asking salary history apply regardless of size in member states. In the UK, the consultation proposes pay information in job adverts with no size threshold confirmed yet. Small employers who sort their bands now do it cheaply; large ones do it under deadline.

What if we just need one number, fast?

That is exactly what a Benchmark is. One role, at least three independent sources, a recommendation you can act on the same week. Most clients start there and come back for bands later.
