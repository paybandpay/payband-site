Payband Benchmark

From £750, fixed fee

What the £750 starting fee covers: one UK role, in one agreed geography, with a clear base salary recommendation.

  • The role matched by scope and accountability, not job title alone
  • At least 3 independent perspectives: official or published data, relevant sector evidence and current market evidence
  • A recommended salary or range, stated in 2 sentences at the top of page 1
  • Every figure footnoted to its source, with the source date, assumptions and limitations made visible
  • A recorded video walkthrough you can replay and share with your board, or a live call if you prefer
  • One written clarification round within 5 working days of delivery

Quoted separately: additional roles or countries, job evaluation, job description rewriting, organisation-wide pay equity analysis and legal advice.

The outcome: you make the pay decision this week, with evidence, instead of guessing or paying an agency to guess for you.

Timeline: 5 working days from briefing.

From £2,500, fixed fee

What you get: a defined reward project, built and handed over. Pick one:

  • Pay bands for your whole organisation, with the method documented so you can maintain them without me
  • A full benchmarking refresh across your roles, with a board ready summary
  • A pay transparency readiness audit: where you stand against the EU directive and UK direction of travel, traffic lighted, with a prioritised action list
  • Benefits benchmarking and pay policy: what you offer against the market, and the written policy that makes it defensible

Reward consultancy questions

What does a salary benchmark include?

The £750 starting fee covers one UK role in one agreed geography, at least three independent perspectives, a recommended salary or range, source footnotes, stated assumptions, a recorded walkthrough or live call and one written clarification round. It is delivered in five working days from briefing.

Can Payband Advisory build pay bands for a whole organisation?

Yes. Payband Build can cover organisation-wide pay bands with the method documented so your team can maintain them. The usual timeline is three to six weeks, depending on scope.

Do you work with UK employers that have staff in the EU?

Yes. Salary benchmarking and reward projects are available UK-wide. Pay transparency readiness work can cover UK employers with staff in EU member states, based on the relevant national rules and their current transposition status.

What does Payband Partner include?

The £8,000 annual starting fee includes up to six single-role UK Benchmarks, one annual pay review cycle, quarterly reward reviews, a written decision log and routine written advice normally answered within two working days. Larger projects and work outside the agreed annual scope are quoted separately.

How do I start a reward consultancy project?

Email a short written brief covering the role or pay question, your organisation and rough headcount, the countries involved and your deadline. You will get a fixed quote and start date by email.
