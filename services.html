Reward consultancy questions

What does a salary benchmark include?

A Payband Benchmark includes at least three independent sources, a recommended salary or range, source footnotes and a recorded video walkthrough or live call. It is delivered in five working days from briefing.

Can Payband Advisory build pay bands for a whole organisation?

Yes. Payband Build can cover organisation-wide pay bands with the method documented so your team can maintain them. The usual timeline is three to six weeks, depending on scope.

Do you work with UK employers that have staff in the EU?

Yes. Salary benchmarking and reward projects are available UK-wide. Pay transparency readiness work can cover UK employers with staff in EU member states, based on the relevant national rules and their current transposition status.

How do I start a reward consultancy project?

Email a short written brief covering the role or pay question, your organisation and rough headcount, the countries involved and your deadline. You will get a fixed quote and start date by email.
