Assumptions and limitations

  • Role is at first hire into the position, not a successor to a previous post-holder.
  • Headcount band assumes no immediate large growth in the team reporting in.
  • No bonus, long-term incentive, pension or benefits included in the base range.
  • North West regional adjustment applied using ONS regional earnings factors.
  • Three sources is the minimum. For a higher-stakes hire or a counter-offer negotiation, a fourth source and a written walkthrough are recommended at the £750 Benchmark tier.
