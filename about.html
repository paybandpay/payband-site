What I will and won't do

Payband Advisory does pay: benchmarking, bands, transparency readiness, reward strategy. Three things I will signpost elsewhere:

  • Payroll operation, payroll system implementation, payroll vendor selection.
  • Legal advice on employment contracts, TUPE, equal pay claims or tribunal work.
  • Board pack rewrites, executive coaching, or anything that needs a soundbite rather than evidence.

How I work
