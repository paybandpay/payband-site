About

Payband Advisory is run by Kieron Power. One adviser, doing the work, not a bench of juniors.

Who you are dealing with

7 years in reward, across Marshalls plc, PwC UK, PwC Middle East, Freshfields and Kennedys.

Manchester based, working UK wide. If you send a brief, this is who reads it and replies. Verify all of this on LinkedIn.

Most recently I designed and led the pay transparency programme at a 2,500-person global law firm, spanning 45+ offices: job architecture, pay banding, gap analysis, bonus modelling the board used, and country by country transposition tracking. Done for real, on live deadlines, not as a thought piece.

Before that I benchmarked pay at two Big 4 firms and ran reward in-house at a FTSE 250 manufacturer. I have sat on both sides: the consultant sending the report, and the person who has to make it work on a payroll budget.

How I work

Fixed fees, agreed before work starts. Sources footnoted on every number. Plain English throughout.

One rule sits over everything that leaves this desk: if a busy HR director cannot act on it in 60 seconds, it gets rewritten.
